# 🎬 *The Marvels* (2023)

> _“We're not a team.” — Kamala Khan, Monica Rambeau, and Carol Danvers_

**The Marvels** is a 2023 American superhero film from the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU). It’s a sequel to *Captain Marvel* and continues the story from the *Ms. Marvel* series.

---

## ⭐ Key Details

- **Directed by:** Nia DaCosta  
- **Produced by:** Kevin Feige  
- **Written by:** Nia DaCosta, Megan McDonnell, Elissa Karasik  
- **Release Date:** November 10, 2023  
- **Runtime:** 105 minutes  
- **Budget:** $274 million  
- **Box Office:** $206.1 million

## 📖 Plot Summary

When Carol Danvers, Monica Rambeau, and Kamala Khan begin mysteriously swapping places every time they use their powers, they must team up to uncover the cause. Together, they form **The Marvels**, a trio of powerful women navigating cosmic threats and personal growth.
